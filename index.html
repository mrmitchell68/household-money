Put it on your home screen

Android Chrome and iPhone Safari

Android Chrome

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮).
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Open Money from your home screen.

iPhone Safari

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The icon is named Money.

Backup

Entries live on this phone. When the phone is online, new income and spending are also added to the shared Google Sheet, under the category you picked. Photos stay on the phone; the sheet stores the receipt file name. If the phone is offline, the entry is saved here and sent when you open the app online again. Export a backup if you want a file of what is saved here. Import merges by entry id and does not duplicate entries already on this phone.

A receipt photo can fill in the amount and store, and this phone remembers which category you used for that store.
