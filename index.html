Put it on your home screen

Android Chrome and iPhone Safari

Android Chrome

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮).
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Open Money from your home screen.

iPhone Safari

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The icon is named Money.

Backup

Income, spending, and categories saved on either phone show up on the other phone when each app is opened online, because both read the shared sheet. When the phone is online, new income and spending are also added to the shared Google Sheet, under the category you picked. Photos stay on the phone that took them. The other phone sees the receipt file name only, and the sheet stores the receipt file name. If the phone is offline, the entry is saved here and sent when you open the app online again. Export a backup if you want a file of what is saved here. Import merges by entry id and does not duplicate entries already on this phone.

A receipt photo can fill in the amount and store, and this phone remembers which category you used for that store.

Savings you put aside or take out is saved the same way and is not counted as spending. What is left after income, spending, and savings carries to the next month, even if it is negative.

Bill reminders are shared the same way. A reminder is not spending until you tap Approve, and you can change the date first.
